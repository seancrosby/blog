## Bio and Hobbies
I am a member of the Computer Science and Engineering faculty at BYU-Idaho. Beyond my professional life as a developer, I am a Christian, husband, and father. My hobbies include mountain biking, hiking, camping, running, and canoeing. I also enjoy playing with my kids, working on remodeling projects, and watching rocket launches.

## Purpose of This Blog
This blog serves a dual purpose: it functions as a model for students building their own online portfolios and as a platform to share insights that help grow faith in Jesus Christ.
