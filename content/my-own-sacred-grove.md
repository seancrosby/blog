In the year 1820, there was a 14-year-old boy in upstate New York who wanted to know what church to join. He went to a grove near his home to ask God which one was right. His simple question initiated the restoration of the gospel of Jesus Christ in our day. Several years ago, I had the opportunity to go to the grove where this boy, Joseph Smith, prayed and had his glorious vision of the Father and the Son. I wanted to go off by myself and ask the question: did it happen here? The answer I received was unexpected but has blessed my life immensely.

I tell my story in this video:

Years later, my children started learning the song "My Own Sacred Grove" in primary at church. I immediately recognized the message of the song as the same thing I had learned that summer morning in the Sacred Grove: we all need somewhere close and convenient where we can go to commune with God. I hope you enjoy the song.
