Here are some of the projects I've been working on:

## [Half Birthday Calculator](https://seancrosby.github.io/half-birthday)
A simple web application to calculate your half birthday. It helps you find that special day exactly six months from your birthday.

*   **Live Site:** [seancrosby.github.io/half-birthday](https://seancrosby.github.io/half-birthday)
*   **GitHub Repositories:**
    *   [half-birthday](https://github.com/seancrosby/half-birthday.git)
    *   [half-birthday-calc](https://github.com/seancrosby/half-birthday-calc.git)

## [Gemini CLI](https://github.com/seancrosby/gemini-cli.git)
My homegrown command-line wrapper for Google's Gemini.  This allowed me to use AI in the terminal.  I switched to the official Gemini CLI after it came out.

*   **GitHub Repository:** [gemini-cli](https://github.com/seancrosby/gemini-cli.git)

## [QR Code Generator](https://seancrosby.github.io/qr-code)
A fast and easy-to-use QR code generator. Input any URL or text and instantly create a QR code that you can download or share.

*   **Live Site:** [seancrosby.github.io/qr-code](https://seancrosby.github.io/qr-code)
*   **GitHub Repository:** [qr-code](https://github.com/seancrosby/qr-code.git)
